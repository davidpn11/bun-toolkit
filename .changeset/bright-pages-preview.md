New `btk md` tool: `btk md README.md` opens a live-reloading preview in the browser, `btk md CHANGELOG.md --terminal | less -R` renders it for the terminal, `btk md serve docs` serves a whole folder with a sidebar, and `btk md html` writes a standalone page. Pick a look with `--theme`; `btk` and `plain` are built in, and `btk md themes` lists them.
