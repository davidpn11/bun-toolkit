btk is now built on Bun 1.4.3, and the binary starts about twice as fast because it is compiled to bytecode. `btk image` reads CMYK and YCCK JPEGs, slightly damaged JPEGs, and 10-bit HEIC files on macOS. `btk convert --to yaml` no longer leaves a trailing space after keys, and with `--indent` it keeps `[]` and `{}` on the key's line and aligns nested lists. `btk md` renders deeply nested lists much faster.
